## What this reading supports

Concrete candidate circumstances differ across cases: premeal direction and baseline (P32/P36), delayed rise with different height (P5/P38 low), timing of activity (P27/P2/P49), and missing activity/history information (P32/P41). These observations neither establish a universal conversion nor establish impossibility. The nutrition-history anomalies are visible in the actual source files; their meaning must be resolved before using those quantities as trusted calibration inputs. The 300/400/500 entries conflict with the dictionary's stated percentage range; do not silently scale macros using them.

The previous exploratory guessed-correction outputs remain separate. They were generated before the user's latest instruction to inspect raw numbers only and are not the evidence for these observations.
