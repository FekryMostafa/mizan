# Fat-sensitive model audit

Read all seven pages of [Noguchi–Furutani 2017](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/iscie/30/7/30_286/_pdf). PDF screenshots failed; equations were inspected as extracted text.

Fat affects glucose through modeled lipid-induced insulin resistance. Gastric delays are fixed, not calibrated functions of fat grams. Validation supplies insulin treatment and lipid measurements; it does not demonstrate unknown-dose recovery. The authors report an early glucose mismatch and require further validation across meal compositions.

**Derived scale ambiguity:** multiplying unknown fat input and lipid distribution volume by the same factor preserves the downstream lipid and glucose trajectories in these equations. Known-dose calibration can break this ambiguity; this is not proof against personalization.

Decision: retain as a mechanism reference, not a ready CGM-only predictor. Missing work: calibrate the fat-response scale, model endogenous insulin, and validate unseen fat doses. No ±10% result established.
